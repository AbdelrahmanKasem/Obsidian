# 📚 Modern Study Techniques (Math & CS)

### 🧠 1. Active Recall

- Close the book.
- Recall from memory.
- Test yourself instead of rereading.

---

### ⏰ 2. Spaced Repetition

- Review after:
    - 1 day
    - 3 days
    - 7 days
    - 14 days
    - 30 days

---

### 🎯 3. Deliberate Practice

- Solve problems that challenge you.
- Focus on weak areas.

---

### 🔀 4. Interleaving

- Mix different topics in one study session.
- Example:
    - Functions
    - Limits
    - Trigonometry
    - Programming

---

### 👨‍🏫 5. Feynman Technique

- Explain the topic simply.
- If you can't explain it, you don't understand it.

---

### 💪 6. Productive Struggle

- Think for 15–30 minutes before looking at the solution.

---

### ❌ 7. Error Log

For every mistake write:

- What was wrong?
- Why?
- Correct solution.
- How to avoid it.

---

### 📄 8. Retrieval Sheet

After studying:

- Take a blank paper.
- Write everything from memory.
- Compare with your notes.

---

### 📖 9. Worked Examples → Practice

Progression:

1. Read example.
2. Similar problem.
3. Different problem.
4. Hard problem.
5. Create your own.

---

### 🖼️ 10. Dual Coding

Use:

- Text + Diagrams
- Equations + Graphs
- Code + Flowcharts

---

### 🗺️ 11. Concept Maps

Connect ideas visually.

Example:

```
Functions
 ├── Polynomial
 ├── Rational
 ├── Exponential
 └── Logarithmic
```

---

### ❓12. Ask "Why?"

Never memorize blindly.

Ask:

- Why does this work?
- Where is it used?
- What happens if...?

---

### 💻13. Build Without Looking

Don't copy tutorials.  
Create projects from memory.

---

### 📚14. Read Documentation

Use official documentation—not only videos.

---

### ✍️15. Daily Problem Solving

Read less.  
Solve more.

---

### 🤖16. Use AI as a Tutor

1. Try first.
2. Ask for a hint.
3. Finish yourself.
4. Review mistakes.

---

### 📈17. Mixed Difficulty

- 60% Easy
- 30% Medium
- 10% Hard

---

### 🔗18. Build Connections

Relate topics.

Example:

- Calculus → Machine Learning
- Matrices → Graphics
- Logarithms → Algorithms

---

### 🔄19. Weekly Review

Every week:

- Review notes
- Redo mistakes
- Find weak points
- Plan next week

---

### 😴20. Sleep & Exercise

- 7–9 hours of sleep.
- Regular exercise improves memory and focus.

---

# ⭐ Core Formula (80/20 Rule)

If you only remember **5 techniques**, make them these:

1. 🧠 Active Recall
2. ⏰ Spaced Repetition
3. 🎯 Deliberate Practice
4. ❌ Error Log
5. 💻 Solve Problems Every Day
