# Guiding Principles

These should guide every semester:

- **University comes first.** A high GPA is one of the strongest factors for competitive scholarships.
- **Master the mathematics, not just the programming.** Your degree's mathematical depth is your competitive advantage.
- **Build continuously.** One quality project each semester is better than many unfinished ones.
- **Read research regularly.** Start with one paper per week in Year 2 and increase as you become more comfortable.
- **Document everything.** Keep organized notes in Obsidian, maintain a polished GitHub profile, and record what you learn.
- **Improve English every week.** Don't leave IELTS preparation until the last minute.
- **Seek opportunities proactively.** Apply for internships, research programs, competitions, and scholarships even if you think they're ambitious.
