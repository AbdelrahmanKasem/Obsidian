## 7. Assessment and credit

- **Accumulative point system.**
    
- **Attendance:** 30 classes = **30 points**.
    
- **Control events:** **70 points**.
    
- **Credit:** need **50–100 points** and completion of all tests in methodical-practical and lecture classes.
    
- **Current control:** testing, oral survey, Kahoot testing, Moodle testing (in the 2025 part-time version).
    
- **Boundary controls:** 3 per semester.
    
- **Credit includes:** fulfillment of control standards, attendance, activity in classes.
    

## 8. What the files collectively say

- All three files describe the same discipline: **«Физическая культура и спорт»** for bachelor’s students.
    
- The 2023 full-time and 2025 part-time versions differ only in formal details: form of study, specific areas, and minor wording.
    
- The content is identical: **14 hours of lectures**, **50 hours of practical classes**, **8 hours of independent work**, **no labs**, and a **credit / зачёт**.
    
- The main focus is on practical physical training, methodical-practical skills, healthy lifestyle, self-control, and physical fitness standards.
    
- The discipline uses a mixed learning format, including in-person sports classes and electronic resources in ОРИОКС, Kahoot, Google Forms, Moodle, YouTube, etc.
