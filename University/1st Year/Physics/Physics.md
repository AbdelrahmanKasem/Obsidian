## **4.1. Lecture Sessions**

### **Module 1: Kinematics, Dynamics of a Material Point, and Conservation Laws**

****Lecture 1 (2 hours): Kinematics of Material Point Motion****

- Physical models: material point, system of material points, and absolutely rigid body
    
- Space and time
    
- Methods of describing the motion of a material point
    
- Displacement, velocity, and acceleration
    
- Normal and tangential acceleration
    

****Lecture 2 (2 hours): Kinematics of Rigid Body Motion****

- Degrees of freedom and generalized coordinates
    
- Number of degrees of freedom of an absolutely rigid body
    
- Vectors of elementary angular displacement, angular velocity, and angular acceleration
    
- Relationship between angular and linear quantities
    

****Lectures 3–5 (6 hours): Dynamics of a Material Point and Conservation Laws****

- Inertial reference frames
    
- Newton's First Law
    
- Galilean principle of relativity
    
- Galilean transformations
    
- Mass and momentum
    
- Newton's Second and Third Laws
    
- Equation of motion of a material point in a rotating non-inertial reference frame
    
- Momentum of a system of material points
    
- Law of conservation of momentum
    
- Center of mass
    
- Theorem on motion of the center of mass
    
- Center-of-mass reference system
    
- Work, power, and energy
    
- Kinetic energy of a system of material points
    
- Theorem on the change of kinetic energy
    
- Conservative and non-conservative forces
    
- Potential energy
    
- Laws of conservation and change of mechanical energy
    
- Angular momentum and torque
    
- Equation of moments
    
- Law of conservation of angular momentum
    

---

### **Module 2: Rigid Body Dynamics, Relativistic Mechanics, Mechanical Oscillations, and Mechanical Waves**

****Lectures 6–7 (4 hours): Rigid Body Dynamics****

- Rigid body as a system of material points
    
- Equations of motion and equilibrium of a rigid body
    
- Rotation of a rigid body about a fixed axis
    
- Moment of inertia
    
- Steiner's theorem (parallel-axis theorem)
    
- Kinetic energy of a rotating rigid body
    
- Work of external forces during rotation of a rigid body
    
- Plane motion of a rigid body
    

****Lecture 8 (2 hours): Fundamentals of Relativistic Mechanics****

- Postulates of the special theory of relativity
    
- Lorentz transformations
    
- Consequences of Lorentz transformations
    
- Twin paradox
    
- Relativistic velocity-addition formulas
    
- Relativistic dynamics
    
- Relativistic momentum and energy
    
- Equation of motion of a relativistic particle
    
- Motion of a particle in a constant force field
    

****Lectures 9–11 (6 hours): Mechanical Oscillations****

- Concept of oscillatory processes
    
- Harmonic oscillations
    
- Amplitude, frequency, and phase of harmonic oscillations
    
- Equation of harmonic oscillations
    
- Oscillations of a load on a spring
    
- Small oscillations of mathematical and physical pendulums
    
- Complex and vector forms of representing oscillations
    
- Addition of oscillations
    
- Beats
    
- Kinetic and potential energy of an oscillating body
    
- Damped oscillations
    
- Damping coefficient, logarithmic decrement, and quality factor
    
- Forced oscillations under a sinusoidal force
    
- Amplitude and phase in forced oscillations
    
- Resonance
    

****Lectures 12–13 (4 hours): Mechanical Waves****

- Waves
    
- Phase velocity and wavelength
    
- Plane and spherical waves
    
- Standing waves
    
- String oscillations
    
- One-dimensional wave equation
    
- Waves in an elastic medium
    
- Energy of an elastic wave
    
- Umov vector
    

---

### **Module 3: Molecular Physics**

****Lecture 14 (2 hours): Molecular-Kinetic Theory of Matter Structure****

- Statistical distributions of gas molecules by velocities and energies
    
- Barometric formula
    
- Boltzmann distribution
    
- Some information from probability theory
    
- Maxwell distribution
    
- Average kinetic energy of molecules
    
- Thermal motion velocities
    

****Lectures 15–16 (4 hours): Thermodynamic Description of Processes****

- Thermal motion of atoms and molecules
    
- Temperature
    
- Equation of state of an ideal gas
    
- Internal energy of a system
    
- First law of thermodynamics
    
- Reversible and irreversible processes
    
- Equilibrium processes in an ideal gas
    
- Efficiency of a heat engine
    
- Order and disorder in nature
    
- Entropy
    
- Second law of thermodynamics
    
- Heat capacity
