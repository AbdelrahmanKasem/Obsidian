## 3. Lecture content

The 2025 all-directions RPD gives the following lecture plan:

|Lecture No.|Hours|Content|
|---|---|---|
|1–2|4|**Современная Россия: цифры и факты.** Symbols of Russia: flag, coat of arms, anthem. Informal symbols. Objective and characteristic data about Russia, its geography, resources, economy. Population, culture, religions and languages. Modern situation of Russian regions.|
|3|2|**Цивилизационный подход к изучению России.** Formational approach. National (constructivist) approach. Origins of the civilizational approach. Modern versions of the civilizational approach.|
|4–5|4|**Россия как цивилизация.** Philosophical understanding of Russian civilizational identity. Institutional configuration of civilization.|
|6–7|4|**Мировоззрение и его структура.** Worldview: essence and structure. Forms of representation of worldview. Worldview and collective identity.|
|8–10|6|**Ценности как смысловая основа мировоззрения.** Values in the subject field of social sciences. Traditional values as the basis of Russian worldview. Worldview and socialization. Axiological foundations of Russian civilization and worldview. Transformation of values of Russian society in the post-Soviet period. Value-worldview attitudes in modern Russian society.|
|11|2|**Социальное служение.** Concept of “social service”. Main directions of service. Motives and incentives for participation in service. History of social service in Russia. Factors determining the modern development of social service. Volunteer service as a direction of social service.|
|12|2|**Основы конституционного строя Российской Федерации.** Power. State. Sovereignty. Republic and democracy. Democratic state. Federal state. Legal state. Republic. Social state. Ideological diversity. Secular state.|
|13|2|**Ветви и уровни публичной власти. Стратегическое планирование в российской политике.** President of Russia. Legislative power. Executive power. Regional executive power. Judicial power. Local self-government. Concept of “strategic planning”. Classification of strategic planning documents. National projects. Forecasting documents: socio-economic development forecast of the Russian Federation; budget forecast of the Russian Federation for the period up to 2036. Planning and programming documents.|
|14|2|**Россия и мир.** Characteristics of the modern system of international relations. CIS. UN. Integration processes in the post-Soviet space. Russia’s mission in the emerging multipolar world. BRICS. OPEC+. Shanghai Cooperation Organization. Russia–Africa. Russia–Latin America.|
|15|2|**Актуальные вызовы и проблемы развития России.** Global problems and Russia’s response to them. Neo-colonialism and globalism. Climate change. Inequality and poverty. Food deficit and depletion of natural resources. Migration and refugees. Crisis of the modern world economy model. Crisis of representative democracy. Scientific and technological revolution and development of digital technologies. Information overload. Digital insecurity. Potentially asymmetric advantages. Internal challenges and response to them. Policy of responding to internal challenges.|
|16|2|**Образ будущего России.** Systemic five-element model of civic worldview: Person. Family. Society. State. Country. Content characteristics and guidelines for the image of the future of the Russian Federation.|

> In the 2024 profile-specific version for 02.03.01 «Математика и компьютерные науки» / «Компьютерная математика и анализ данных», the lecture list is shorter (14 lectures) and the modules are 5, but the main topics overlap.

## 4. Practical class content

There are **no laboratory works**. Instead, the discipline has **practical classes**. The 2025 all-directions RPD lists 8 practical classes:

| Practical No. | Hours | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1             | 2     | **Многообразие российских регионов.** Presentations by students about the features of their native city and region, answers to students’ questions, free discussions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 2             | 2     | **Российская цивилизация в исторической динамике.** Discussion (within business games and scenario techniques) of the natural-geographical factor in the development of Russian civilization (Mechnikov, Milov), historical-institutional effects within the socio-cultural development of Russian civilization.                                                                                                        |
| 3             | 2     | **Российская цивилизационная идентичность на современном этапе.** Discussion of current sociological data on Russian identity. Interactive educational technologies (discussions, debates) tied to key positions about the present and future of Russian civilization, mechanisms of support for the existing civilizational heritage, etc.                                                                             |
| 4             | 2     | **Ценностные вызовы современной политики.** Discussions, case studies and work with empirical (sociological) data within problem-based learning connected with the features of modern public opinion and public consciousness. Identification of key value challenges, description of their effect on the transformation of society, power and state, presentation of results through quizzes, quests and competitions. |
| 5             | 2     | **Ценности российской цивилизации.** Reports and presentations on key value principles of Russian civilization. Viewing and discussion of multimedia materials.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 6             | 2     | **Добровольческое (волонтерское) служение как направление социального служения.** Reports and presentations on the development of volunteering in Russia, principles of volunteer activity, volunteer organizations, and work with the Internet platform [dobro.ru](https://dobro.ru/).                                                                                                                                 |
| 7             | 2     | **Планирование будущего: национальные проекты и государственные программы.** Case studies related to priorities of long-term development of the country, development and implementation of strategies and programmes, features of national projects.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 8             | 2     | **Россия и глобальные вызовы.** Business games on identifying challenges, discussions and debates on the list of global problems of priority importance for Russia. Work with students.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
