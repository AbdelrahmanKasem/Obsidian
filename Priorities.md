# 🎯 Priority 1 — Academic Excellence (Foundation)

> **Without this, everything else becomes much harder.**

## Goals

- Graduate with an excellent GPA (top of the class if possible).
- Master every university subject.
- Develop deep mathematical understanding.

### Focus

- Attend lectures
- Review notes daily
- Solve extra exercises
- Ask professors questions
- Prepare well before exams

# 📌 Your Guiding Principle

Whenever you have free time and aren't sure what to work on, ask yourself:

> **"Which of these priorities will have the greatest impact on my long-term goal of becoming an AI researcher and earning a top master's scholarship?"**

If you consistently choose the highest-priority item available, you'll naturally make steady progress without feeling overwhelmed.
